Call for Papers: CVPR 2027

3D Geometry Generation for Scientific Computing: Agentic 3D/4D Modeling for Scientific Simulation and Design

OpenReview submission portal: 3D4S at CVPR 2027

Scope

We invite research on agents that reconstruct, generate, or refine 3D structures and time-varying 3D models for scientific inference, physical simulation, or function-driven design. Work should identify a scientific objective beyond visual appearance and explain how agent actions or feedback contribute to it.

Topics include measurement-grounded reconstruction, geometry and meshing for solvers, inverse design, dynamic physical identification, scientific tool orchestration, and evaluation under uncertainty. See the full topic list. We welcome rigorous negative results, benchmarks, and reproducible scientific software as well as new methods and applications.

Publication and format

The workshop is non-archival. Accepted papers will be made available through OpenReview with author consent under the announced submission terms; they will not appear in IEEE/CVF proceedings.

Papers will follow the CVPR 2027 submission style and applicable format and length restrictions. Regular papers must contain 4-8 content pages, excluding references and appendices. Submissions must be anonymized, including supplementary material.

Review process

Papers receive double-blind review with three independent reviews per paper. Reviewers will assess relevance, correctness, scientific evidence, reproducibility, and clarity. Each author may be listed on at most ten submissions to this workshop, as required by CVPR 2027. Authors and reviewers must declare conflicts of interest.

Authors should report the scientific question, geometric representation, agent actions and tools, physical assumptions, feedback signals, computational cost, human intervention, and failure modes. Claims should distinguish appearance agreement, geometric validity, numerical physical validity, and independent validation. Preliminary work is welcome when claims match the available evidence.

All accepted papers will be offered poster presentations. A subset will be selected for contributed talks. The workshop does not include a competition or challenge.

Data, reproducibility, and responsible use

Document data permissions and licensing, protect sensitive anatomy or environments, and label synthetic measurements. State model limitations and avoid presenting simulated results as experimental validation. Share code, configurations, and agent/tool traces where permitted; describe constraints on release when sharing is not possible.

Conference requirements

Participation is in person, with recording and remote-access arrangements provided by CVPR. All participants must register in advance. See the official workshop requirements.
